Child Combinator (>)

The selector .demo-box > p selects only paragraphs that are direct children of .demo-box.

Paragraph 1: direct child — selected by both descendant and child selectors.

Paragraph 2: nested inside a section — selected by the descendant selector, but NOT by the child selector.

Paragraph 3: direct child — selected by both selectors.

Paragraph 4: outside .demo-box — not selected by either selector.
